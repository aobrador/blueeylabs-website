Privacy Policy

Your privacy is fundamental to how Blufinn was designed.

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Effective upon download and use of Blufinn

Our Commitment

Blufinn was built with a privacy-first philosophy. We do not collect your personal financial data. We do not sell your data. We do not track you. This policy explains exactly what we do and don't do.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

Blufinn does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following on our servers:

  • Credit card numbers, account numbers, or banking credentials
  • Bill amounts, due dates, or payment history you enter
  • Budget categories, line items, or spending data
  • Paycheck amounts or income information
  • Subscription or recurring bill details
  • Any financial information you input into the App

2. Where Your Data Lives

All data you enter into Blufinn is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage. Blueey Labs has no access to this data — it never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable iCloud sync.

Optional iCloud Sync

If and when iCloud sync is made available, your data will sync across your personal Apple devices via Apple's iCloud service. This sync is end-to-end encrypted by Apple. Blueey Labs does not have access to your iCloud data. Your use of iCloud is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Ask Blufinn (On-Device Assistant)

The Ask Blufinn feature is a local assistant that uses only the data already stored on your device to answer questions. It does not connect to the internet, does not transmit data to external servers, and does not use third-party AI services. Everything runs on your device.

4. Device Permissions

Blufinn may request the following permissions:

  • Notifications — to alert you of upcoming and overdue payments. This is optional and can be disabled in iOS Settings at any time.
  • iCloud — only if you choose to enable optional sync. Not required to use the App.

We do not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or any other sensitive device capabilities.

5. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Blufinn may use Apple's built-in, privacy-preserving analytics tools (such as App Store analytics) which provide aggregated, anonymized data about app usage. No personally identifiable information is collected through these tools. You can opt out of this in your iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

We do not use third-party analytics SDKs (such as Firebase, Mixpanel, or similar services).

6. No Account Required

Blufinn does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or share any personal information to use the App.

7. Children's Privacy

Blufinn is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. The App is intended for general personal finance planning use by adults.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and notify users through an in-app notice or App Store update notes. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Your Rights

Because all your data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over it at all times. You can delete your data by deleting the App from your device. There is no account to delete and no data to request from us, because we don't have any.
