Privacy Policy

Your privacy is fundamental to how Blufinn was designed.

Last updated: November 2026  ·  Effective upon download and use of Blufinn

Our Commitment

Blufinn was built with a privacy-first philosophy. We do not collect your personal financial data. We do not sell your data. We do not track you. This policy explains exactly what we do and don't do.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

Blufinn does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following on our servers:

  • Credit card numbers, account numbers, or banking credentials
  • Bill amounts, due dates, or payment history you enter
  • Budget categories, line items, or spending data
  • Paycheck amounts or income information
  • Subscription or recurring bill details
  • Any financial information you input into the App

2. Where Your Data Lives

All data you enter into Blufinn is stored on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud account. It is never sent to Blueey Labs. We do not operate servers that receive or store your data, and we have no access to it.

iCloud Sync

iCloud sync is on by default. If your device is signed in to iCloud and iCloud is enabled for Blufinn, your data syncs across your own Apple devices through Apple's iCloud service and is stored in your private iCloud storage, which only your Apple Account can access. Apple protects iCloud data in transit and on its servers. Blueey Labs cannot see or retrieve your iCloud data. Your use of iCloud is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sync is controlled by your device's iCloud settings, not by a switch inside the App. Blufinn shows an iCloud Sync status page, and you can turn sync off at any time in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud, by switching off Blufinn in the list of apps using iCloud. If you are not signed in to iCloud, or you turn sync off, new changes stay only on that device. Turning sync off does not by itself delete data already stored in your iCloud account. See "Your Rights" below for how to remove it.

Card Catalog

Blufinn includes a catalog of credit cards with general details such as rewards rates and annual fees. This is public reference information that Blueey Labs gathers from publicly available sources and includes inside the app itself. Catalog changes reach you through App Store updates. The app does not contact a Blueey Labs server to download the catalog, and nothing you have entered, including which cards you own, is sent to us or to anyone else. If you personalize a saved card, those edits are part of your own data and are stored the same way as everything else you enter.

3. Ask Blufinn (On-Device Assistant)

The Ask Blufinn feature is a local assistant that uses only the data already stored on your device to answer questions. It does not connect to the internet, does not transmit data to external servers, and does not use third-party AI services. Everything runs on your device.

4. Device Permissions

Blufinn may request the following permissions:

  • Notifications — to alert you of upcoming and overdue payments. This is optional and can be disabled in iOS Settings at any time.
  • iCloud — used to sync your data across your own devices when you are signed in to iCloud. You can turn this off in iOS Settings. iCloud is not required to use the App.

We do not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or any other sensitive device capabilities.

5. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Blufinn may use Apple's built-in, privacy-preserving analytics tools (such as App Store analytics) which provide aggregated, anonymized data about app usage. No personally identifiable information is collected through these tools. You can opt out of this in your iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers.

We do not use third-party analytics SDKs (such as Firebase, Mixpanel, or similar services).

6. No Account Required

Blufinn does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or share any personal information to use the App.

7. Children's Privacy

Blufinn is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. The App is intended for general personal finance planning use by adults.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and notify users through an in-app notice or App Store update notes. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Your Rights

Because your data is stored only on your device and in your own iCloud account, you have complete control over it at all times. Deleting the App removes its data from that device. To also remove data stored in iCloud, go to iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage (or Storage), select Blufinn, and delete its data. There is no Blufinn account to delete and no data to request from us, because we don't have any.
