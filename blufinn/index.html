FAQ

Questions.

When can I download Blufinn?

Blufinn is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone. Send us a message and we'll let you know when it launches.

Do I have to connect my bank account?

No. Blufinn never connects to your bank. You enter your cards, bills and paychecks yourself.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Blueey Labs has no access to it. If you turn on optional iCloud sync, your data syncs across your own Apple devices through Apple's iCloud.

What is Ask Blufinn?

An assistant built into the app that answers questions using only the data already on your device. It doesn't connect to the internet.

Does Blufinn give financial advice?

No. Blufinn is a personal planning tool that helps you organize your own information. It isn't financial, investment, tax or legal advice.
