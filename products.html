Blufinn

Know what's due. Know what's left.

Blufinn is a private, local-first money planner for credit cards, recurring bills, budgets, and paychecks. It helps you track what's due, organize what each paycheck needs to cover, and see what money you have left — with complete clarity and without linking your bank accounts.

💳

Credit Card Tracking

Due dates, statement dates, annual fees, rewards, and monthly credits — all in one place.

💵

Paycheck Planning

Assign bills, card payments, and recurring expenses to each paycheck so nothing slips through.

🔁

Recurring Hub

Subscriptions and fixed monthly bills organized in a single, clear recurring expenses view.

📊

Budget View

Categories, line items, and remaining balances so you always know exactly where you stand.

🔔

Smart Alerts

Surfaces upcoming and overdue payments before they become a problem.

🤖

Ask Blufinn

A local assistant that answers your financial questions using only the data inside your app.
