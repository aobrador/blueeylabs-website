The fee problem

Every platform takes a different cut.

eBay, Whatnot and TCGPlayer each charge their own mix of percentages and per-order fees. Working that out sale by sale in a spreadsheet is slow and easy to get wrong. StockLore does that math for you on every sale, so you just see what you made.

Who it's for

Built by a seller, for sellers.

StockLore comes out of running Blueey Collects: buying collections and sealed product, selling on eBay, Whatnot, TCGPlayer and in person, and needing one place that shows what's left after cost and fees.

  • Trading-card businesses and resellers
  • Sellers of singles and sealed product
  • Anyone selling on more than one platform
  • Collectors who want to know what their collection is worth
  • Anyone tired of tracking cards in a spreadsheet
