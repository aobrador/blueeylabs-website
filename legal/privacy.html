Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you use our website.

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Applies to blueeylabs.com

1. Overview

Blueey Labs, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy describes what limited information we collect through blueeylabs.com and how we use it. We keep it simple: we collect very little, and we don't sell anything.

2. Information We Collect

Contact Form Submissions

When you submit a message through our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and the content of your message. This is processed through Formspree, a third-party form service. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry.

Basic Usage Data

Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may collect standard web server logs including IP addresses, browser type, and pages visited for security and performance purposes. We do not use this data for advertising or tracking.

3. What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information
  • We do not use tracking pixels, third-party advertising SDKs, or behavioral analytics
  • We do not run retargeting campaigns using your data
  • We do not require account creation to use this Site

4. Cookies

This Site does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Basic session functionality may use temporary browser storage that is cleared when you close your browser.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Cloudflare — DNS, hosting, and security. Subject to Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Formspree — Contact form processing. Subject to Formspree's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — Web fonts. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

Contact form messages are retained only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry. We do not maintain long-term marketing lists from contact form submissions.

7. Your Rights

You may request deletion of any contact information you've submitted by emailing us at info@blueeylabs.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at info@blueeylabs.com.
