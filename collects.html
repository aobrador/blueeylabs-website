Selling FAQ

Good to know.

Where can I sell Lorcana cards in Orlando?

Right here. Blueey Collects buys Lorcana cards from sellers across the Orlando area and Central Florida, with local meetups, and from anywhere else by mail.

What do you buy?

Disney Lorcana singles, sealed product, full collections and bulk lots.

Is there a minimum?

No. Whether it's a few cards or an entire collection, send it over and I'll take a look.

How do I get an offer?

Message @blueeycollects on Instagram with photos or a list of what you have. I'll look it over and reply with a price. There's no obligation to accept.

How do you pay?

Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, or cash in person.

I'm not in Orlando. Can I still sell to you?

Yes. Local sellers can meet up in the Orlando area, and everyone else can ship. Shipping is worked out as part of each deal.
