How it works

About the math.

How is profit calculated?

Sale price plus shipping charged, minus platform fees, minus what you paid for the item, minus your own shipping cost. Margin is that profit divided by the sale price plus shipping charged.

How are eBay fees calculated?

A final value fee percentage on the sale price plus shipping charged, plus a small per-order fee that depends on whether the order is over $10. eBay also charges its percentage on the sales tax the buyer pays, which this calculator leaves out, so real fees run slightly higher.

How are Whatnot fees calculated?

A commission percentage on the sale price, plus payment processing (a percentage and a flat amount) on the sale price plus shipping charged. Processing also applies to sales tax, which isn't included here.

How are TCGPlayer fees calculated?

A commission percentage on the sale price plus shipping charged, up to a cap, plus payment processing (a percentage and a flat amount). The flat processing amount is per order, so it hurts most on cheap single-card orders.

Why does selling in person show no fees?

Cash deals at a show or local meetup have no platform fee. If you take payment through an app that charges business fees, count that as part of your shipping and supplies cost.

Does this work for sealed product?

Yes. The math is the same for singles, sealed boxes and lots. Shipping cost matters much more for sealed, so fill that in.
